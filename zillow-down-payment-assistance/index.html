What do you still owe?

A plain-English guide to the new 3.5% FHA assistance offer. Understand the money, the five-year commitment, and the bills that remain.

The key distinction: assistance starts as debt.

It can disappear if you fulfill the forgiveness requirements. Until then, it is another loan secured by your home. Lower upfront cash does not prove the monthly payment fits your budget. [1, 2]

01 / Start here

Two loans. Two different jobs.

Your main FHA mortgage

The big loan used to buy the house. FHA is a government insurance program for participating lenders. You still owe principal, interest, and mortgage insurance, alongside taxes and homeowners insurance.

Your assistance loan

Zillow Home Loans provides up to 3.5% of the lower of the price or appraisal. This second mortgage has 0% interest and no monthly installment. It is conditionally forgiven after five years. [1, 2]

Down payment = the purchase money the first mortgage does not cover. Appraisal = the lender's assessment of the property's value. A second mortgage gives another creditor a legal claim against the home.

02 / Check the fit

Credit score alone is not approval.

640+

Program credit minimum

Use an FHA purchase loan from Zillow Home Loans for a home you live in. Underwriting still checks your income, debts, and ability to pay. [2]

No cap

On income

The announcement lists no income ceiling and allows repeat buyers. Enough qualifying income is still necessary. Buyers may add their own 6.5% for a total 10% down. [2]

5 years

Of conditions

Owner occupancy and on-time mortgage payments are required for forgiveness. Obtain the actual assistance agreement before committing. [2]

State availability has a published inconsistency.

Both Zillow pages exclude AZ, DE, FL, MA, NY and VA. The FAQ also excludes Vermont; the launch announcement instead excludes Rhode Island. Confirm either state directly; do not treat a missing exclusion as approval. [1, 2]

For H-1B and other nonpermanent residents

HUD removed FHA eligibility for nonpermanent resident borrowers effective May 25, 2025. An H-1B visa or work authorization by itself does not establish FHA eligibility. Lawful permanent residents can qualify under the applicable rules. This FHA-based offer therefore generally will not work for someone relying only on H-1B status. Ask about conventional financing separately. [5]

03 / Make it concrete

What happens on a $500,000 home?

Change the assumptions. All figures below are educational estimates, not Zillow rates, fee quotes, or an eligibility decision. Nothing is submitted or stored.

Your example

Assumes appraisal equals price, maximum assistance, 3.5% down, 30-year fixed mortgage, and financed upfront mortgage insurance. Closing-cost input excludes that financed premium; include cash-paid prepaids/escrow in your own estimate.

The purchase math

Closing costs are a planning allowance. Inspection, moving, repairs, appraisal gaps, or lender-required reserves may need extra cash. Credits and deposits already paid affect the actual amount due at closing.

Your estimated monthly housing bill

Mortgage insurance uses an approximate first-year monthly average based on base principal. Actual premiums depend on FHA's balance method. Utilities, repairs, maintenance, and special assessments are extra.

Show the formulas and mortgage insurance assumptions

Assistance = price × 0.035. Base mortgage = price × 0.965. Upfront mortgage insurance = base mortgage × 0.0175, rounded down to cents and added to the mortgage here. Payment = L × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−360], where L is the financed mortgage and r is annual interest ÷ 12. At 0% interest, payment = L ÷ 360.

Approximate monthly FHA insurance = base mortgage × annual MIP rate ÷ 12. For this 30-year, 96.5% loan-to-value example, the rate is 0.55% at a base loan of $726,200 or less, and 0.75% above. Upfront MIP is 1.75%. [3] Property tax = price × tax rate ÷ 12. Cash comparison = down payment + the entered closing allowance, before credits, deposits, and additional costs.

An appraisal gap needs separate money.

If the price is $500,000 but the appraisal is $480,000, maximum assistance is $16,800, not $17,500. At 96.5% of appraised value, the base first mortgage is $463,200. Together they cover $480,000; the remaining $20,000 price gap needs another solution, such as a price reduction or your cash. Closing costs are extra. This is simplified appraisal math, subject to lender review.

Can you use it for a $500,000 home?

Potentially. With the assumptions above, the base mortgage is $482,500. That is below the 2026 one-unit FHA floor of $541,287. Passing a loan limit does not mean you qualify; location, property, borrower eligibility, and program approval still matter. Higher-cost counties can have higher limits. [6]

04 / Understand the commitment

Year four is very different from year five.

Moving out, selling, or refinancing before five years triggers full repayment under the announcement. There is no published promise of gradual forgiveness. [2]

Thinking of refinancing?

This example ignores refinance fees, changes in loan balance, interest, and time value. It illustrates the repayment hurdle; it is not a refinance recommendation.

Assistance forgiveness does not end FHA mortgage insurance.

On a new FHA loan starting above 90% loan-to-value, annual MIP generally lasts for the mortgage term. Starting at 90% or below generally means 11 years. Reaching 20% equity later does not automatically remove FHA MIP. [4]

05 / FAQ, translated

The questions a new buyer should ask.

Covers all ten FAQ topics on Zillow's program page, with related explanations above. Short summaries below refer to that FAQ. [1]

What is the assistance actually paying?

Your down payment, through a second mortgage. It does not reduce the home's sale price.

How much down is needed, and how much is covered?

FHA's minimum here is 3.5%; assistance can cover it. The lower appraisal or price determines the maximum. The calculator shows the dollars.

Is it a grant? Must I repay it?

A conditional loan. Forgiveness requires five years of compliance; earlier sale or refinance requires repayment.

Does it cover closing costs?

No. Budget separately. Zillow estimates 2–5% of the loan amount.

Will it slow down closing?

The program is integrated into Zillow's loan process. Its FAQ says to plan at least 21 days from rate lock to closing; that is not a deadline guarantee.

Where is it offered?

See the state discrepancy above. Verify your property location before relying on the offer.

Must this be my first home?

No. Repeat buyers may qualify.

Does 0% mean my whole mortgage has no interest?

No. That applies to the assistance loan. The first mortgage has its own rate and monthly payment. [2]

Is this automatically the cheapest mortgage?

Insufficient evidence. The public announcement does not establish that its total cost beats competing loans. Compare actual Loan Estimates: rate, APR, points, lender fees, mortgage insurance, cash due, and five-year borrowing costs. APR includes certain financing charges; it is broader than the interest rate. [7]

What is not answered clearly in the public FAQ?

Late-payment cures, exact forgiveness processing, special exceptions, partner-specific conditions, and whether other assistance can be combined need written confirmation. Ask for both loan documents. Do not invent favorable terms where the page is silent.

06 / Your next move

Compare the whole deal before applying.

Check the gates first.

Confirm state, residency eligibility, property type, county loan limit, and expected time living in the home.

Get the terms in writing.

Ask: “What triggers repayment? What counts as on time? How do I obtain forgiveness and release the second lien?” A lien release removes the creditor's claim against the property.

Request competing Loan Estimates.

Compare with and without assistance and with any conventional options you qualify for. Use the same loan size, lock period, and date where possible. Include any expected assistance repayment in an early-exit scenario. [7]

Stress-test your monthly budget.

Add maintenance and utilities. Ask what happens if income stops or you must relocate. Keep emergency savings after closing. Assistance solves one upfront hurdle; it does not measure affordability.

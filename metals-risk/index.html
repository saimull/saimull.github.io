How this works

RSI over 14 available observations (20%), extension above the mean of available prices in the past 200 calendar days (30%), elapsed-time-adjusted sampled volatility over 30 days (25%), and gold/silver ratio percentile over three calendar years (25%). Ratio prices use the latest available other-metal observation, at most seven days old. Older history is sampled, so these are exploratory indicators rather than standard daily technical indicators; scores can differ with sampling density.

This is an exploratory composite, not a calibrated probability of loss. The weighting and thresholds have not been statistically validated. Do not use it alone to time trades or invest.

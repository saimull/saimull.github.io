How this works

RSI (20%), 200-day moving-average extension (30%), rolling realized volatility (25%), and gold/silver ratio percentile (25%). The ratio factor is inverted for gold versus silver to reflect relative strength. Inputs are scaled to 0–1 using fixed rules or trailing history, without future data. Volatility measures uncertainty, not direction.

This is an exploratory composite, not a calibrated probability of loss. The weighting and thresholds have not been statistically validated. Do not use it alone to time trades or invest.

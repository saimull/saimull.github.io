How this works

Both scores use RSI over 14 available observations (20%), extension relative to the mean of available prices in the past 200 calendar days (30%), and elapsed-time-adjusted sampled volatility over 30 days (25%).

Gold uses its own decline from the highest sampled close in the past 60 calendar days for the remaining 25%. This factor rises from 0 at the peak to 1 at a 20% decline and captures recent losses that can persist even after RSI and price extension fall.

Silver uses the inverse gold/silver ratio percentile over three years for the remaining 25%. It pairs each silver price with the latest available gold price, up to seven days old. Older history is sampled, so the scores can differ with sampling density.

This is an exploratory composite, not a calibrated probability of loss. The weighting and thresholds have not been statistically validated. Do not use it alone to time trades or invest.

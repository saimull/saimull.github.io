Best order to negotiate

  1. Identify the exact home, lot, construction stage, and builder deadline
  2. Negotiate price, lot premium, and high-dollar credits first
  3. Compare builder financing against an outside lender using the same loan assumptions
  4. Ask for upgrades and move-in extras that cost you more than they cost the builder
  5. Get every promise in the signed contract or addendum before paying a deposit

Ask this directly

  • “What additional incentive is available on this exact home if I sign by your deadline?”
  • “Can I use the incentive toward price, closing costs, rate, or upgrades—and which gives me the highest total value?”
  • “Which fees or deposits are nonrefundable, and under what exact conditions?”
  • “Please show every incentive and included upgrade in writing before I sign.”

Notes & assumptions

Investing the difference: Early on, renting may cost less than owning, so the calculator assumes the renter invests those monthly savings. Eventually, owning the home may become cheaper as rent continues to increase while mortgage principal and interest remain fixed—and eventually disappear when the mortgage is paid off. At that point, the calculator assumes the homeowner invests the monthly savings instead. These investments are included in each side's final net worth.

Calculation method: Mortgage amortization and investment growth are calculated monthly. Rent and homeowners insurance increase once every 12 months. Home appreciation uses the selected annual assumption converted to a monthly equivalent rate. Property tax and maintenance are estimated from modeled home value.

Mortgage insurance uses the monthly amount entered and remains in the model unless set to $0. Closing costs and selling costs are excluded. HOA fees, investment taxes, income-tax effects, mortgage-interest deductions and other tax benefits/costs are excluded.

If “invest avoided down payment” is enabled, the renter's investment portfolio begins with the down payment the buyer would have used.

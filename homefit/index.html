Clear assumptions. No black box.

This prototype uses deterministic calculations and hypothetical data. The score weights cash flow (50%), three months of essential reserves (40%), and housing share of take-home pay (10%). It is an illustrative planning aid, not a validated risk model, credit score, lending decision, or personalized financial recommendation.

What’s included — and what comes next

Housing includes principal and interest, taxes, insurance, HOA, maintenance and estimated PMI below 20% down. Cash after closing subtracts down payment and closing costs. Essential reserves include all modeled housing costs plus essential nonhousing spending (capped at total nonhousing spending). Moving, renovations, special assessments, tax effects and lender-specific costs are excluded. Rates and costs are editable assumptions, not live quotes.

Bank connections, CSV analysis and AI categorization are planned features and are not active in this demo. Nothing entered here is sent to a server or saved by this app. Do not enter account numbers or upload financial statements.

FREE FIRST-TIME HOMEOWNER CHECKLIST

The Real Cost of Your First Home

20 expenses new homeowners often underestimate.

Buying the house is just the beginning. Your mortgage payment isn't your entire housing budget. Some costs are essential, others optional. You don't need to buy everything at once.
  1. 01
    Property tax increases

    Tax bills may change after reassessment or exemptions change.

  2. 02
    Homeowners insurance increases

    Premiums and coverage costs can rise at renewal.

  3. 03
    HOA fees and special assessments

    Dues may increase; some communities charge special assessments.

  4. 04
    Lawn care and landscaping

    Mowing, equipment, trimming, seasonal cleanup, and landscaping.

  5. 05
    Pest control

    Preventive treatments and occasional infestations.

  6. 06
    HVAC maintenance and repairs

    Filter changes, servicing, and eventual repairs.

  7. 07
    Plumbing and electrical repairs

    Leaks, clogs, outlets, fixtures, and service calls.

  8. 08
    Appliance replacements

    Budget for replacing equipment as it wears out.

  9. 09
    Furniture and mattresses

    Start with what you need rather than filling every room.

  10. 10
    Curtains, blinds, and window treatments

    Privacy coverings are easy to overlook at move-in.

  11. 11
    Washer, dryer, and refrigerator

    Check which appliances actually come with the home.

  12. 12
    Lawn mower and household tools

    Basic maintenance gear adds up, even when purchased gradually.

  13. 13
    Security cameras and smart home devices

    Optional subscriptions, cameras, doorbells, and connected devices.

  14. 14
    Patio furniture and backyard upgrades

    Grills, outdoor seating, and landscaping upgrades can wait.

  15. 15
    Decorations and seasonal purchases

    Small décor and holiday purchases can become a recurring habit.

  16. 16
    Higher utility bills

    Larger spaces and different utility responsibilities can raise bills.

  17. 17
    Moving costs and supplies

    Truck rentals, packing supplies, deposits, and setup fees.

  18. 18
    Unexpected repairs and emergency savings

    Keep a cash buffer for repairs you cannot predict.

  19. 19
    Fence installation or replacement

    Installation, gates, permits, staining, and future repairs; check HOA rules.

  20. 20
    Whole-house water filtration system

    Upfront equipment and installation plus filter changes and servicing; test your water before buying.

Don't spend your full approval amount just because you can.

Leave room for ongoing ownership costs, unexpected repairs, and the purchases you'll be tempted to make after moving in. A mortgage payment may already include escrowed taxes and insurance, but those costs can still increase.

Not every expense applies to every home. Costs depend on location, property condition, climate, HOA rules, and your preferences. Educational information only, not individualized financial advice.
